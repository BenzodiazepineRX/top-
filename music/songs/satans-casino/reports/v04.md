# Satan's Casino v04

Baseline is v03 (`satans-casino-v03-review.wav`), the mix Chex said sounded amazing. Dry beat -8 dB and dry vocal -6 dB are the v03 buses, unchanged.

No listening was done for this pass. A speech model supplied word times. Peaks are not approval.

6–12 kHz in the beat during the quiet hole is about -80 dB after the -8 dB fader. The vocal in that hole is about -79 dB up there. That is not a hiss floor. During the vocal, 6–12 kHz is the beat's hats. Those were left alone. The extra 2–5 kHz in the hole is the reverse swell. The extra 2–5 kHz in the intro is the telephone copy. Only those two buses were darkened. No gate.

Intro 2–5 kHz, telephone bus: -55.47 dB before, -56.1 dB after a 2.5 dB cut above 3.2 kHz.
