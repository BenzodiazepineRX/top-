## Creative effects requests

List every effect the owner wants, and the exact moment it happens. An effect can sit on **one track** (a vocal, a snare, a drum bus) or on the **whole mix** (the mix bus or master), so the entire song moves together.

Agents acknowledge this list in `mix-notes.md`, then build each line. How the common effects are made, including whole-mix moves, is in `music/effects-guide.md`.

If a plugin is named, use that plugin. Leave a row as written until the owner changes it. If this song has no special effects, write "none for this song."

Name the bar, the lyric, or the hit, and say how many bars the move lasts. "Somewhere in the chorus" is not enough.

One-track examples: a reverse reverb into one word, an echo throw on a phrase, a vocal that fades in filtered and distorted and then gets louder and clearer, a riser on a noise track.

Whole-mix examples, automated on the mix bus over a set number of bars: the entire song fading in filtered, distorted, and muffled, then opening up louder and clearer; a full-mix filter sweep; a drop; a transition into the chorus; a tape stop into silence; a beat switch at a bar number.
